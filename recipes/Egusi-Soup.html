Egusi soup Recipes

Ingredients:

  • Egusi seeds (melon seeds) - 2 cups
  • Assorted meat (beef, goat meat, or chicken) - 1.5kg
  • Stockfish - 1 medium size
  • Dry fish - 1 medium size
  • Palm oil - 250ml
  • Ground crayfish - 1 cup
  • Vegetables (spinach, bitterleaf, or ugu) - a bunch
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Water
Serving: 6-8 people

Steps:

  1. Wash and season the assorted meat with salt and pepper. Boil until tender.
  2. Soak the stockfish and dry fish in warm water for about 10 minutes, then wash and set aside.
  3. Grind the egusi seeds into a fine powder using a blender or mortar and pestle.
  4. In a large pot, heat the palm oil over medium heat. Add the ground egusi and stir continuously for about 5 minutes until it thickens.
  5. Add the boiled meat, stockfish, and dry fish to the pot. Stir well to combine.
  6. Add water gradually to achieve your desired soup consistency. Bring to a boil.
  7. Add the ground crayfish. Stir well.
  8. Wash and chop the vegetables, then add them to the soup. Cook for an additional 5-10 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  9. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Serve hot with your choice of swallow (fufu, pounded yam, etc.).
