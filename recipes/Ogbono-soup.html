Ogbono soup Recipes

Ingredients:

  • Assorted meat 1.5kg
  • 2 cups of ogbono seeds
  • Stockfish head (medium size)
  • 1 medium-size dry catfish
  • 1 cup of crayfish (ground)
  • Fluted pumpkin (a bunch)
  • 1 teaspoon of ogiri or ground dawadawa or okpei (local ingredients) (optional)
  • 250ml of palm oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Water
  • Salt
Serving: 6-8 people

Steps:

  1. Wash and season the assorted meat with salt and pepper. Boil until tender.
  2. Soak the stockfish and dry catfish in warm water for about 10 minutes, then wash and set aside.
  3. Grind the ogbono seeds into a fine powder using a blender or mortar and pestle.
  4. In a large pot, heat the palm oil over medium heat. Add the ground ogbono and stir continuously for about 5 minutes until it thickens.
  5. Add the boiled meat, stockfish, and dry catfish to the pot. Stir well to combine.
  6. Add water gradually to achieve your desired soup consistency. Bring to a boil.
  7. Add the ground crayfish and optional ogiri/dawadawa/okpei. Stir well.
  8. Wash and chop the fluted pumpkin leaves, then add them to the soup. Cook for an additional 5-10 minutes until the leaves are tender.
  9. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Serve hot with your choice of swallow (fufu, pounded yam, etc.).
