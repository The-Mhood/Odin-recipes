Edikaikong soup Recipes

Ingredients:

  • Edikaikong leaves
  • Water leaves
  • Meat (beef, goat meat, or chicken)
  • Stockfish
  • Dry fish
  • Palm oil
Serving: 6-8 people

Steps:

  1. Wash and season the meat with salt and pepper. Boil until tender.
  2. Soak the stockfish and dry fish in warm water for about 10 minutes, then wash and set aside.
  3. In a large pot, heat the palm oil over medium heat. Add the boiled meat, stockfish, and dry fish to the pot. Stir well to combine.
  4. Add water gradually to achieve your desired soup consistency. Bring to a boil.
  5. Wash and chop the edikaikong leaves and water leaves, then add them to the soup. Cook for an additional 5-10 minutes until the leaves are tender.
  6. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Serve hot with your choice of swallow (fufu, pounded yam, etc.).
